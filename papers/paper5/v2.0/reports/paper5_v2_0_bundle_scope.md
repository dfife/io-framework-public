# Paper 5 v2.0 Bundle Scope

This bundle supports the rebuilt Paper 5 v2.0 manuscript. It is a calculation
and audit-support bundle, not an independent proof engine.

The bundle reproduces the local Paper 5 arithmetic for Kerr spin and the
active-branch BBN expansion-rate comparison. It also records the Vaidya
null-dust no-go diagnostic in a machine-readable form and freezes the inherited
dependency pointers used by the manuscript ledger.

The light-element scorecard itself belongs to Papers 22 and 24. Paper 5 uses
those results only to state the consistency boundary: BBN abundances are
improved by rate-dressing perturbations to nuclear cross-sections, while the
active-branch background expansion rate remains near standard.
