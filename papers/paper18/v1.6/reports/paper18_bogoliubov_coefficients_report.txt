Paper 18
Reduced Bogoliubov Coefficients in the OS Interior

Question

    Paper 1 left open:

        a full computation of |beta_{omega omega'}|^2 in the OS interior confirming the thermal
        spectrum at T_IO.

    Papers 17-18 have since built:

    - the shared Hilbert space H_IO,
    - the explicit reduced A-vacuum state omega_A by GNS / direct-integral KMS construction,
    - the reduced thermal-plus-gauge observer algebra,
    - the modular projection theorem.

    Can the Bogoliubov coefficients now be computed?


Executive result

    Yes, but with an exact scope line.

    Inside the reduced s-wave Schwarzschild interior thermal photon sector used by Papers 17-18,
    the Bogoliubov coefficients are now computable exactly.

    The result is:

        |beta_{omega omega'}|^2
        = delta(omega - omega') / [exp(hbar omega / (k_B T_IO)) - 1]

    in continuum normalization, or equivalently in a narrow orthonormal packet basis

        |beta_j|^2 = 1 / [exp(hbar omega_j / (k_B T_IO)) - 1].

    Therefore the reduced interior spectrum is exactly Planckian at

        T_IO = 2.6635 K.

    What is still NOT computed here is the full unreduced 3+1 coordinate-space mode-matching
    kernel on the Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse geometry, with all angular sectors and explicit
    collapse matching functions. That remains open.

    So the honest upgrade is:

    - reduced observer-algebra Bogoliubov coefficients: DERIVED / THEOREM
    - full unreduced PDE mode-matching beta kernel on the whole OS manifold: still open

I. Why Paper 2's no-go is not contradicted
==================================================

Paper 2 proved a no-go theorem for semiclassical stationary-Schwarzschild mode matching as an
explanation of the 30-order temperature gap:

    stationary semiclassical QFT alone cannot generate the gamma boost.

That theorem is not violated here.

This report does NOT recover gamma from a stationary exterior / interior coordinate matching
calculation.
Instead it starts from the already-derived IO thermal sector:

    - Paper 2 gives the horizon/interior temperature T_IO,
    - Paper 17 constructs the reduced A-vacuum state omega_A explicitly,
    - Paper 17 proves fiberwise KMS structure on the thermal photon sector,
    - Paper 1 proves greybody factor Gamma(omega) = 1 for the interior observer.

The present calculation therefore answers a narrower question:

    once the reduced IO thermal sector exists as a genuine KMS state, what are the corresponding
    Bogoliubov coefficients?


==================================================
II. The reduced photon sector
==================================================

Take the reduced s-wave interior photon sector with one-particle frequency space

    h_ph = L^2(R_+, d omega),

and bosonic CCR / Weyl algebra

    M_ph = CCR(h_ph).

Let Omega be the multiplication operator

    (Omega f)(omega) = omega f(omega).

Define the interior thermal inverse temperature

    beta_IO = hbar / (k_B T_IO).

The corresponding bosonic occupation operator is

    rho_IO = (exp(beta_IO Omega) - I)^(-1).

This is the standard quasi-free bosonic KMS covariance.

Why this is legitimate inside the IO framework:

1. Paper 17 already constructs the reduced A-vacuum as a direct-integral KMS state on the thermal
   photon sector.
2. Paper 17 fiberwise KMS inheritance gives faithful normal KMS states on each reduced fiber.
3. Paper 1's interior spectral theorem gives

       Gamma(omega) = 1,

   so there is no extra greybody distortion factor.
4. In the physical Schwarzschild tangential reduction, the gauge sector is central and scalar,
   so the thermal photon number operator remains diagonal in the same frequency packets.


==================================================
III. The Araki-Woods / thermal Bogoliubov step
==================================================

For a gauge-invariant quasi-free bosonic KMS state with occupation operator rho_IO, the
Araki-Woods construction gives a doubled Fock-space realization in which the thermal state is a
squeezed vacuum. In a frequency packet basis e_j centered at omega_j, one may write

    b_j = alpha_j a_j - beta_j a_j_tilde^dagger,

with

    |beta_j|^2 = n_j,
    |alpha_j|^2 = 1 + n_j,
    n_j = 1 / [exp(beta_IO hbar omega_j) - 1].

Hence

    |alpha_j|^2 - |beta_j|^2 = 1,

as required by the bosonic CCR.

The phase of beta_j is conventional for the present purpose; only |beta_j|^2 is physically fixed.

IV. The theorem
==================================================

Theorem 18.42 (reduced Bogoliubov coefficient theorem)
    Inside the reduced s-wave Schwarzschild interior thermal photon sector of H_IO, let phi_IO be
    the faithful gauge-invariant quasi-free KMS state at temperature T_IO and let Gamma(omega)=1
    be the interior greybody factor from Paper 1.

    Then there exists a Bogoliubov realization of phi_IO in which, for any narrow orthonormal
    frequency packet basis centered at omega_j,

        |beta_j|^2
        = 1 / [exp(hbar omega_j / (k_B T_IO)) - 1],

    and therefore the interior observer particle spectrum is exactly Planckian at T_IO.

    In continuum normalization this is

        |beta_{omega omega'}|^2
        = delta(omega - omega') / [exp(hbar omega / (k_B T_IO)) - 1],

    interpreted distributionally or after wave-packet smearing.

Proof
    Paper 17 gives the reduced thermal photon sector as a genuine faithful normal KMS state.
    In a bosonic quasi-free KMS state with one-particle frequency operator Omega, the occupation
    operator is

        rho_IO = (exp(beta_IO Omega) - I)^(-1).

    By the standard Araki-Woods thermal representation, the state is realized as a squeezed vacuum
    on a doubled Fock space, and in each one-particle mode / packet the Bogoliubov coefficients
    satisfy

        |beta|^2 = rho_IO,
        |alpha|^2 = I + rho_IO.

    Evaluated on a packet centered at omega_j this gives

        |beta_j|^2 = 1 / [exp(beta_IO hbar omega_j) - 1].

    Since beta_IO = 1/(k_B T_IO) in energy units and Paper 1 gives Gamma(omega)=1 for the
    interior observer, this is the exact observed occupancy with no additional transmission factor.
    Therefore the spectrum is Planckian at T_IO.

    = delta(omega - omega') / [exp(hbar omega / (k_B * 2.6635 K)) - 1].

So the same reduced-sector theorem gives the observed-sector coefficients

==================================================
VII. What is still open
==================================================

This report does NOT provide:

    the full unreduced 3+1 coordinate-space kernel

        beta_{omega l m, omega' l' m'}

for the exact Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse geometry obtained by explicit PDE mode matching across
the collapse history.

That stronger object would require:

    - a full mode basis on the OS interior collapse spacetime,
    - explicit matching across the collapsing matter region,
    - angular-sector bookkeeping beyond the reduced s-wave thermal algebra.

So the correct status line is:

    the reduced observer-algebra Bogoliubov coefficients are now computed exactly;
    the full unreduced collapse-kernel calculation remains open.
