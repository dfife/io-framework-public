V. Effective mode count theorem
==================================================

Definition 18.36 (effective mode count in the reduced scalar class)
    Let C_ac be the normalized reduced acoustic covariance kernel on the commutative scalar cell
    algebra. Define the effective mode count by the entropy rank

        N_eff(C_ac) := exp(S(C_ac)),

    where S(C_ac) is the Shannon / von Neumann entropy of the normalized spectrum of C_ac.

Remark
    This is the correct noninteger category for the Paper 11 quantity N_modes: it is an effective
    multiplicity of correlated acoustic degrees of freedom, not a literal topological rank.

Theorem 18.37 (uniform-spectrum entropy-rank theorem)
    For a commutative normalized covariance kernel with flat spectrum on a set of total measure M,
    the effective mode count defined above equals M.

Proof
    A flat normalized spectrum on total measure M has density

        rho = 1 / M

    on its support.

    Therefore its entropy is

        S(C_ac)
        = - integral rho ln rho d mu
        = - integral (1/M) ln(1/M) d mu
        = ln M.

    Exponentiating,

        N_eff(C_ac) = exp(S(C_ac)) = exp(ln M) = M.

QED.
