Paper 18
CMP Upgrade Theorem

Question

    Upgrade the Conformal Modular Principle (CMP) from a semiclassical principle to a theorem using
    the shared-Hilbert-space / GNS / modular machinery built through Paper 17.


Executive result

    CMP can be upgraded to theorem-grade inside the reduced observer algebra already isolated by
    Papers 14-17.

    The key move is to stop treating the geometric term

        K_geom = 4 ln x

    as an unexplained c-number and instead realize it as the logarithm of a commutative relative
    modular operator on an abelian gravitational history algebra.

    Then the Paper 17 reduced gauge modular operator supplies

        K_hat_g = K_gauge I.

    Because the geometric history algebra is central and the reduced gauge sector is independent,
    the total modular operator factorizes and the modular Hamiltonian adds:

        K_tot = K_geom I + K_hat_g.

    On the physical Schwarzschild tangential sector this gives

        <K> = 4 ln x + ln(1 + gamma^2)
            = ln[x^4 (1 + gamma^2)]
            = ln Delta.

    Strongest honest status:

        DERIVED / THEOREM inside the reduced Schwarzschild tangential observer algebra.

==================================================
II. Geometric history algebra
==================================================

Definition 18.1 (abelian conformal-history algebra)
    Let X be the coarse-grained sample space of one conformal step of interior OS histories.
    Define the commutative von Neumann algebra

        M_geom := L^\infty(X, m),

    where m is a fixed reference measure on history cells.

Interpretation
    This is the geometric sector that counts macroscopic history cells per conformal step. Because
    it is a counting / measure sector rather than a nonabelian field algebra, it is commutative.

III. Unique local extensive geometric weight
==================================================

Proposition 18.2 (unique zero-derivative local measure density)
    On a 4-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime, any local diffeomorphism-covariant extensive
    measure density built algebraically from the metric alone and carrying density weight one is,
    up to an overall constant,

        sqrt(-g) d^4x.

Proof
    A local extensive measure must assign to each infinitesimal history cell a scalar density of
    weight one.

    If no extra fields, couplings, or derivative terms are inserted, the only tensorial input is the
    metric g_mu_nu. A scalar density of weight one built algebraically from the metric alone must be
    proportional to sqrt(-g).

    The constant prefactor cancels in all modular ratios.

Comment
    This is exactly the class that survives the Paper 10 no-go scan: local QFT / determinant /
    anomaly routes either vanish or give the wrong power, leaving the pure geometric 4-volume
    density as the unique local extensive survivor.

Corollary 18.3 (OS conformal scaling)
    In the OS conformal form

        g = a(eta)^2 g_tilde,

    one has

        sqrt(-g) = a(eta)^4 sqrt(-g_tilde).

    Therefore the geometric history weight per conformal step scales as a(eta)^4.

IV. The geometric modular operator
==================================================

Definition 18.4 (epoch weights)
    For each epoch eta define the faithful normal semifinite weight on M_geom by

        Phi_eta(f)
        := integral_X f(x) a(eta)^4 dm(x).

    In particular, at the horizon / turnaround epoch eta = 0,

        Phi_0(f)
        := integral_X f(x) a(0)^4 dm(x).

Theorem 18.5 (geometric relative modular operator)
    On the commutative history algebra M_geom, the Connes-Radon-Nikodym / relative modular
    derivative of Phi_0 with respect to Phi_eta is multiplication by

        d Phi_0 / d Phi_eta
        = a(0)^4 / a(eta)^4
        = x(eta)^4.

    Hence the geometric relative modular Hamiltonian is

        K_geom(eta)
        := ln(d Phi_0 / d Phi_eta)
        = 4 ln x(eta).

Proof
    Because M_geom is commutative, the relative modular operator is just the ordinary
    Radon-Nikodym derivative of the two equivalent measures.

    Since

        d Phi_0 = a(0)^4 dm,
        d Phi_eta = a(eta)^4 dm,

    their Radon-Nikodym derivative is the constant

        a(0)^4 / a(eta)^4.

    Taking the logarithm gives

        K_geom(eta) = ln[a(0)^4 / a(eta)^4] = 4 ln x(eta).

Conclusion
    The old CMP geometric term is now a genuine relative modular generator on an explicit abelian
    sector.

Clarification
    Because M_geom is abelian, its Tomita-Takesaki automorphism on the algebra is trivial. The
    nontrivial object used here is the relative modular operator between Phi_0 and Phi_eta, whose
    logarithm is the geometric relative modular Hamiltonian K_geom.

VI. CMP theorem on the total reduced observer algebra
==================================================

Definition 18.6 (total reduced observer algebra)
    Define

        M_CMP := M_geom bar-tensor M_red.

    Use the product weight/state

        Omega_CMP := Phi_eta bar-tensor omega_A

    on M_CMP, where omega_A is the explicit reduced A-vacuum state constructed in Paper 17.

Theorem 18.7 (CMP upgrade theorem)
    In the reduced Schwarzschild tangential observer algebra M_CMP, the total modular operator
    relative to the horizon geometric weight and the reduced gauge sector is

        Delta_CMP
        = (d Phi_0 / d Phi_eta) bar-tensor Q_hat
        = x(eta)^4 bar-tensor Q_hat.

    Consequently the total modular Hamiltonian is

        K_CMP
        = ln Delta_CMP
        = K_geom(eta) I + K_hat_g.

    On the physical Schwarzschild tangential sector,

        K_CMP
        = [4 ln x(eta) + ln(1 + gamma^2)] I,

    so

        <K>
        = K_geom(eta) + K_gauge
        = ln[x(eta)^4 (1 + gamma^2)]
        = ln Delta.

Proof
    By Theorem 18.5,

        Delta_geom = d Phi_0 / d Phi_eta = x(eta)^4.

    By the Paper 17 reduced gauge theorem,

        Delta_gauge = Q_hat = exp(K_hat_g).

    The geometric history algebra is abelian and central relative to the reduced gauge sector, so
    the total modular operator on the tensor product factorizes:

        Delta_CMP = Delta_geom bar-tensor Delta_gauge.

    Taking the logarithm of commuting positive operators gives

        ln Delta_CMP
        = ln Delta_geom bar-tensor I + I bar-tensor ln Delta_gauge
        = K_geom(eta) I + K_hat_g.

    On the physical reduced Schwarzschild sector,

        K_hat_g = K_gauge I = ln(1 + gamma^2) I.

    Therefore

        K_CMP = [4 ln x(eta) + ln(1 + gamma^2)] I.

    Exponentiating,

        Delta_CMP
        = exp(K_CMP)
        = x(eta)^4 (1 + gamma^2)
        = Delta.

QED.

VII. Interpretation
==================================================

What has changed relative to Paper 10
    Paper 10 had already isolated the unique surviving geometric weight and excluded the standard
    local QFT alternatives, but it still treated the final identification

        K_geom = 4 ln x

    as a semiclassical principle.

    Paper 18 removes that gap by constructing:

    - an explicit commutative geometric history algebra,
    - explicit faithful normal semifinite epoch weights on it,
    - the relative modular operator between those weights.

Why this is enough
    Once the geometric part is realized as a relative modular operator, and the Paper 17 reduced
    gauge modular operator is already theorem-grade, the additive split

        <K> = K_geom + K_gauge

    is no longer a principle. It is the logarithm of a factorized modular operator on the reduced
    observer algebra.
