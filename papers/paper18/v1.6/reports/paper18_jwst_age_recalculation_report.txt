==================================================
II. Friedmann model used
==================================================

Use the mixed-fluid closed-FRW form already adopted after Paper 5:

    H(z)^2
    = H_0^2 [ Omega_r (1+z)^4
            + Omega_m (1+z)^3
            + Omega_k (1+z)^2
            + Omega_Lambda ].

The cosmic age at redshift z is

    t(z)
    = integral_z^infinity dz' / [ (1+z') H(z') ]

or equivalently

    t(a)
    = integral_0^a da' / [ a' H(a') ],
    a = 1/(1+z).
