Paper 25 - Weak Two-Time Thermal Correlator
=========================================

Question
--------

Evaluate the genuine thermal weak-rate kernel

    C(t) = omega_pre(W_np^*(t) W_np(0))

for

    W_np = sigma_pn tensor a_e^*(f_e) a_nu(f_nu) tensor b_w,
    b_w  = a^*(F_0) + a(F_0),

and determine whether the full two-time rate is linear or quadratic in the
bridge amplitude.

Constants
---------

- gamma = 0.2375
- K_gauge = 0.05487281774291466
- Q_hat = 1 + gamma^2 = 1.05640625

I. Factorization of the two-time correlator
-------------------------------------------

On the minimal enlarged weak algebra one has the factorized packaging

    M_w^ext = A_rxn tensor-bar M_rad^BBN tensor-bar W_br,1.

Under the corresponding product/quasi-free KMS packaging,

    C(t)
      = omega_pre(W_np^*(t) W_np(0))
      = C_rxn(t) C_CAR(t) C_br(t),

with

    C_rxn(t) = omega_rxn(sigma_np(t) sigma_pn(0)),
    C_CAR(t) = omega_CAR([a_nu^* a_e](t) [a_e^* a_nu](0)),
    C_br(t)  = omega_br(b_w(t) b_w(0)).

The reaction and CAR legs are gamma-trivial on the current stack, so the only
possible gamma-dependence sits in the bridge factor.

II. Bridge two-time correlator
------------------------------

For quasi-free CCR bridge dynamics on one-particle space,

    b_w(t) = a^*(U_t F_0) + a(U_t F_0),

where U_t is the one-particle unitary flow. Expanding gives

    C_br(t)
      = omega_br(a^*(U_t F_0) a^*(F_0))
      + omega_br(a^*(U_t F_0) a(F_0))
      + omega_br(a(U_t F_0) a^*(F_0))
      + omega_br(a(U_t F_0) a(F_0)).

In the gauge-invariant quasi-free bosonic class the anomalous pairings vanish,
so

    omega_br(a^*(U_t F_0) a^*(F_0)) = 0,
    omega_br(a(U_t F_0) a(F_0))     = 0.

Using

    omega_br(a^*(f) a(g)) = <g, G_gamma^(1) f>,
    omega_br(a(f) a^*(g)) = <f, (G_gamma^(1) + I) g>,

one obtains

    C_br(t)
      = <F_0, G_gamma^(1) U_t F_0>
        + <U_t F_0, (G_gamma^(1) + I) F_0>.

This is bilinear in F_0 for every t.

III. Consequence for gamma-dependence
-------------------------------------

Write

    F_0 = alpha(gamma) F_tilde.

Then

    C_br(t; gamma) = alpha(gamma)^2 C_tilde_br(t; gamma),

and therefore the full rate has the structure

    Gamma_w(gamma)
      = alpha(gamma)^2
        integral dt C_rxn(t) C_CAR(t) C_tilde_br(t; gamma) K_cons(t),

so

    Gamma_w(gamma) / Gamma_w(0)
      = [alpha(gamma)^2/alpha(0)^2] * R(gamma),

with

    R(gamma)
      = [integral dt C_rxn(t) C_CAR(t) C_tilde_br(t; gamma) K_cons(t)]
        /
        [integral dt C_rxn(t) C_CAR(t) C_tilde_br(t; 0) K_cons(t)].

Thus:

- derived: the two-time thermal weak rate is quadratic in the bridge amplitude
  at all time separations;
- derived: there is no linear-in-F_0 term inside the centered two-time KMS
  correlator;
- conditional: if the compared bridge covariance and bridge dynamics are
  gamma-blind on the active shell, then R(gamma) = 1 and the full rate ratio is
  exactly

      1 + gamma^2 = exp(K_gauge).

IV. What remains open
---------------------

The current stack gives:

- vector transport U_t is gamma-blind;
- but the one-particle bridge covariance G_gamma^(1) is not proved gamma-blind
  on the special n=1 shell;
- therefore R(gamma) is not fixed by the present theorem stack.

So the strongest honest theorem is:

- derived: the genuine centered two-time thermal correlator is always quadratic
  in the bridge amplitude;
- conditional: the exact weak-rate ratio is

      Gamma_w(gamma)/Gamma_w(0) = (1 + gamma^2) R(gamma),

  with R(gamma) = 1 if the compared active-shell bridge covariance is
  gamma-blind.

V. Comparison to Paper 22
-------------------------

Because the centered two-time object has no linear-in-F_0 term, the present
Paper 22 first-order weak law

    deltaGamma_w/Gamma_w = 2 Re(delta M_w / M_w) + O(delta omega^2)

cannot be the literal centered-two-time KMS rate. The two belong to different
observable grammars unless a new identification theorem is proved.

Verdict
-------

- derived: the full two-time thermal correlator keeps the weak bridge dependence
  quadratic for all t.
- conditional: if the compared active-shell covariance is gamma-blind, the
  rate ratio is exactly 1 + gamma^2.
- not derived: the present stack does not yet prove that gamma-blindness, nor
  that the centered two-time correlator is the operative Paper 22 weak
  observable.
