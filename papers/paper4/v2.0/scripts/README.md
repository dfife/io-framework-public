# Scripts

- `01_horizon_connections.py`: recomputes Paper 4 horizon constants, dark-energy density routes, MOND-scale acceleration, and records the Paper 2 v2.0 gamma bridge.
- `02_projected_age_and_curvature.py`: recomputes projected optical ages at `z = 6, 10, 14, 20` and records the curvature diagnostic plus Paper 35 flat-CPL reinterpretation.
- `03_inherited_claims.py`: freezes the inherited Paper 28, Paper 32, Paper 24, and Paper 20 values reported by Paper 4 v2.0.
- `04_validate_expected_outputs.py`: regenerates all result JSON and validates expected outputs.
