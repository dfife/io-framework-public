## FIRAS CMB Temperature Datum

- Source: Fixsen, D. J. (2009), "The Temperature of the Cosmic Microwave Background", ApJ 707, 916.
- Manuscript value used by Paper 17 v1.5: `T_FIRAS = 2.7255 +/- 0.0006 K`.
- Role in Paper 17 v1.5: empirical observer-side thermal datum fixing the unique
  optical readout normalization `R4_FIRAS` inside the IO readout family.
- Boundary: FIRAS is not used as evidence that IO independently predicted the
  observed CMB temperature.
