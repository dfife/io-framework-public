I. Setup

I.1 Thermal photon algebra
    Let M_th be the von Neumann algebra of thermal photon observables in the conformal
    frequency variable

        nu = ln omega.

    By Paper 13, exact thermal transfer preserving Planck form acts by uniform translation in nu.
    Therefore the one-particle generator is

I.2 Reduced gauge center

I.3 Physical GNS state
    Let

        M_th bar-tensor Z_g.

    By the premises of this round, the A-vacuum GNS state is already constructed.


II. Standard operator-algebra input

Input T1 (KMS states are modular and determine their modular group uniquely)
    For a faithful normal KMS state, the GNS modular group is exactly the KMS dynamics and is
    unique.

    Source:
    - Derezinski-Pillet, "Tomita-Takesaki theory"
      https://pillet.univ-tln.fr/data/pdf/Tomita-Takesaki_theory.pdf
      Theorem 8, lines 81-85:
      a faithful normal state is a KMS state for its modular group, and the modular group is the
      only dynamics for which the state has this property.

Input T2 (direct-integral modular decomposition)
    The modular operator of a direct integral of standard subspaces decomposes into the direct
    integral of the modular operators of the fibers.

    Source:
    - Longo et al., "Modular Operator for Null Plane Algebras in Free Fields"
      https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9474644/
      lines 591-592 and 701-701.


III. Central disintegration of the thermal sector

Lemma 17.21 (central decomposition over K_hat_g)
    Since Z_g = W*(K_hat_g) is abelian and central on the thermal photon sector, the GNS
    representation of

        M_th bar-tensor Z_g

    decomposes as a direct integral over the spectrum of K_hat_g:

        H_A = integral direct sum H_kappa d mu(kappa),
        pi_A = integral direct sum pi_kappa d mu(kappa),
        Omega_A = integral direct sum Omega_kappa d mu(kappa),
        K_hat_g = integral direct sum kappa I_(H_kappa) d mu(kappa).

Proof
    This is the standard central decomposition for an abelian von Neumann algebra acting
    centrally.


IV. Fiber dynamics

Lemma 17.22 (fiber transfer law)
    On each fiber H_kappa, the thermal photon modular action is the one-parameter dilation group
    generated by

        kappa D.

Proof
    Paper 13 already proves the KMS-rigidity theorem: exact thermal transfer preserving exact
    Planck form must act by uniform frequency rescaling, hence by translation in nu generated by D.

    On the fiber with gauge eigenvalue kappa, the Paper 17 conformal-cell normalization says that
    one conformal e-fold carries the cell weight

        exp(kappa).

    Therefore the transfer law on that fiber is

        nu -> nu + kappa lambda,

    whose generator is kappa D.


V. Modular projection theorem

Theorem 17.23 (GTTP modular projection theorem)
    Let Delta_phys be the modular operator of the GNS A-vacuum state on the thermal photon sector
    algebra

        M_th bar-tensor Z_g.

    Then

        Delta_phys^{it} |_(thermal photon sector)
        = integral direct sum exp(it kappa D) d mu(kappa)
        = exp(it (D tensor K_hat_g)).

Proof
    By Lemma 17.21, the thermal photon sector decomposes over the spectrum of the central gauge
    operator K_hat_g.

    By Lemma 17.22, on the fiber H_kappa the state Omega_kappa is a KMS state for the dilation
    dynamics generated by kappa D.

    By Input T1, the modular group of the fiber state is uniquely that KMS dynamics:

        Delta_kappa^{it} = exp(it kappa D).

    By Input T2, the modular operator of the direct integral state decomposes into the direct
    integral of the fiber modular operators. Therefore

        Delta_phys^{it}
        = integral direct sum Delta_kappa^{it} d mu(kappa)
        = integral direct sum exp(it kappa D) d mu(kappa).

    Since

        K_hat_g = integral direct sum kappa I d mu(kappa),

    functional calculus gives

        integral direct sum exp(it kappa D) d mu(kappa)
        = exp(it (D tensor K_hat_g)).

    This is the claimed equation.


VI. Physical Schwarzschild specialization

Corollary 17.24
    On the physical reduced Schwarzschild tangential horizon sector of Papers 14-16,

        Q_hat = (1 + gamma^2) I,
