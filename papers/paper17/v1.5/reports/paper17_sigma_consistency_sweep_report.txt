Paper 17 - GTTP Self-Consistency Sweep
=========================================

Objective
---------
Treat the GTTP transfer exponent sigma as a free parameter in

    T_obs = T_IO x^sigma

and test whether cross-observable IO self-consistency uniquely selects

    sigma = K_gauge = ln(1 + gamma_BI^2),

or whether a continuous sigma band survives once the full validated Paper 12-14 scorecard is
imposed.

Validation Set
--------------
The main sweep uses the paper-frozen validation scorecard from Papers 12-14. That keeps the
comparison internally consistent with the published IO pipeline. This choice does not weaken the
result, because the core statement proved here is a dependency statement: outside FIRAS, the
published observable set is sigma-blind.

Main Result
-----------
Inside the published IO observable-class architecture, only the thermal observable T_obs depends
on sigma. The validated observables H0, theta*, D/H, Y_p, and the published BAO statistics are
all sigma-blind. Therefore the global self-consistency sweep does NOT uniquely select
sigma = K_gauge; it leaves exactly the FIRAS-allowed interval in sigma.

Dependency Audit
----------------
- H0_P10: sigma_sensitive=False. Composite energy+curvature observable; no GTTP dependence in Papers 10-14. [Paper 14 scorecard / Paper 10 projection]
- H0_Schur: sigma_sensitive=False. Later alternative branch; also sigma-blind. [Paper 11 Schur-based implementation]
- theta_star: sigma_sensitive=False. Acoustic-sector observable fixed by N_eff = Δ and baryon dictionary. Paper 13 does not transfer GTTP into the perturbation sector. [Paper 12 / Paper 14 scorecard]
- D_over_H: sigma_sensitive=False. BBN bulk microphysics uses the BDP baryon density and derived T_IO; Paper 12 explicitly audits this sector as independent of the T_CMB transfer law. [Paper 12 / Paper 14 scorecard]
- Y_p: sigma_sensitive=False. Same BBN-sector independence as D/H. [Paper 12 / Paper 14 scorecard]
- BAO_chi2_P12: sigma_sensitive=False. Published validated BAO statistic; no GTTP dependence in the Paper 12 observable-class architecture. [Paper 12 final scorecard]
- BAO_chi2_split_P11: sigma_sensitive=False. Later alternative BAO branch; also sigma-blind. [Paper 11 v1.1 split-BAO update]

Why the Sweep Does Not Collapse to a Point
------------------------------------------
1. FIRAS constrains sigma continuously through T_obs = T_IO x^sigma.
2. The Paper 12 BBN sector is built from omega_b,geom, F, alpha=1, and T_IO; it does not use
   T_obs or sigma.
3. The Paper 12/14 acoustic sector (theta*, R) is tied to N_eff = Delta and omega_b,eff. Paper 13
   explicitly refuses to transfer GTTP into the perturbation sector.
4. The Paper 10/11/14 H0 chain is composite in the energy and curvature sectors; GTTP is not in
   that assembly map.
5. The validated BAO statistics are bulk / ruler observables in the published observable-class
   split, again independent of sigma.

Conclusion
----------
This sweep is a no-go for uniqueness. Cross-observable self-consistency does not pick out a
single sigma. It only says:

    sigma must lie in the FIRAS-allowed interval.

Hence sigma = K_gauge is not selected by the Paper 12-16 scorecard sweep alone. Its special
status must come from the Paper 17 theorem route, not from a global empirical elimination of
nearby sigma values.
