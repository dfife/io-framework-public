paper-native dimensionless scalars plus multiplicative / logarithmic operations up to bounded

Interpretation
--------------
Raw bounded grammar does produce many accidental in-band composites. That is expected once a
finite constant set is closed under bounded elementary operations. After quotienting by published
paper identities and discarding tautological near-equalities, the framework yields only one
structural survivor class:

    K_gauge.

Exact structural aliases found in Papers 10-16:

    K_gauge
    ln(1 + gamma_BI^2)
    ln(Q)
    ln(C2_ratio)
    K_total - K_geom
    ln(Delta) - K_geom
    ln(Delta / exp(K_geom))
    ln(Delta / x^4)
    V(alpha_BI)

Representative accidental near-hits are saved in the JSON summary. They are mostly products,
ratios, or powers that keep K_gauge almost unchanged by multiplying it by numbers numerically
close to 1. They do not define a second paper-native sigma class.

The structural pattern is that every real survivor reduces to the same gauge quadratic invariant:

    1 + gamma_BI^2

and its logarithm.
