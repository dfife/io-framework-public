# External Dependencies

There are no runtime external data dependencies.

The mathematical dependencies are literature dependencies cited in the Paper
27 v2.0 manuscript and in the reports directory:

- Hodge theory on compact manifolds.
- Compact `SU(2)` representation theory and Schur's lemma.
- Canonical commutation relation algebra and quasi-free KMS state uniqueness.
- Passivity and equilibrium-state uniqueness in algebraic quantum statistical
  mechanics.
- Hawking radiation on a Schwarzschild horizon.

These are cited as standard physics/mathematics inputs, not shipped as data.
