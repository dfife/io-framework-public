## Not Included As Executable Scripts

Not included as public executable scripts:

- private exploratory route scans;
- superseded YPBBN wrapper paths;
- dead-route grids that are no longer active claims;
- full PRyMordial source or external code;
- manuscript drafts and scratch notes.

Rationale: the public bundle is for reproducibility of active Paper 22 claims,
not a dump of the private lab history. Dead routes remain documented in the
paper and audit reports.

## Gaps

No live Paper 22 v1.5/v1.6 number with a public-script expectation was found
without a reproducibility path in this bundle.

The main limitation is intentional: PRyMordial-backed abundance rows are frozen
with provenance rather than rerun, because PRyMordial is a separate repository
and is not redistributed in IO paper bundles.
